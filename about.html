Who is Sybil Kailena?

Born on October 1, 1994, in Kyiv, Ukraine, as Olga Anatolyevna Magdebura, Sybil Kailena is a Ukrainian glamour model, actress, and icon. Before entering the spotlight, she studied economics at KROK University, earning a bachelor's degree in 2013 and briefly working as a real estate appraiser and bank customer operator.

Discovered in 2014 by glamour photographer Arkisi, she quickly made her mark in the modeling world, debuting with MET-Art under the names Kailena and Davina E. Her captivating presence led her to pose for Playboy in 2016, and by January 2017, she made her debut as an adult film actress.
